# The Rudrayya Oil Mill — From Seed to Pure Oil

A luxury editorial, highly interactive website designed for **The Rudrayya Oil Mill**, an authentic traditional homemade cooking oil brand producing 100% pure, slow-pressed artisanal oils (Virgin Coconut Oil, Heritage Groundnut Oil, Wood-Pressed Sesame Oil, and Cold-Pressed Mustard Oil).

---

## Brand Essence & Creative Concept
* **Brand Name**: **The Rudrayya Oil Mill**
* **Core Philosophy**: *"From Seed to Pure Oil"*
* **Heritage Narrative**: Unhurried traditional craftsmanship using aged Vaagai hardwood *Marachekku* (wooden ghani) rotating at under 14 RPM (<45°C ambient friction), single-estate sun-cured copra and heirloom seeds, and unbleached cotton muslin gravity filtration.
* **Aesthetic**: Warm ivory, natural beige, coconut brown, botanical leaf green, earthy charcoal noir, and radiant golden amber oil caustics.

---

## Key Features & Interactive Architecture

1. **HERO SECTION ("Pureness in Every Drop")**
   - Cinematic scroll-driven transformation sequence with live telemetry and interactive stage scrubber:
     - Stage 01: Selected Whole Coconut
     - Stage 02: Cracking & Pure White Copra Reveal
     - Stage 03: Traditional Vaagai Wood Oil Press (Marachekku)
     - Stage 04: Golden Oil Flowing (<45°C)
     - Stage 05: Viscous Amber Decanting Stream
     - Stage 06: Final Apothecary Bottle Settled with The Rudrayya Oil Mill Artisanal Label
   - Ambient golden oil droplets canvas physics simulation with interactive cursor repulsion.

2. **SECTION 2: HORIZONTAL STORYTELLING ("From Nature to Your Kitchen")**
   - Desktop GSAP ScrollTrigger horizontal pinning:
     - 01: Carefully Selected (A+ Grade sun-dried Malabar copra & heirloom groundnuts)
     - 02: Naturally Prepared (Sun-curing on reed mats in village courtyards)
     - 03: Traditionally Pressed (Aged Vaagai hardwood pestle at 14 RPM)
     - 04: Naturally Filtered (72-hour earthenware gravity settling & unbleached muslin)
     - 05: Purely Bottled (Hand-filled apothecary glass, natural cork, and wax seal)
   - Dynamic animated progress indicator bar: `01 → 02 → 03 → 04 → 05`.

3. **SECTION 3: INTERACTIVE OIL COLLECTION**
   - High-fashion editorial product cards:
     - The Rudrayya Virgin Coconut Oil
     - The Rudrayya Pure Groundnut Oil
     - The Rudrayya Wood-Pressed Sesame Oil
   - 3D bottle tilt on hover, glowing colored ambient auras, floating seeds/particles, and instant pantry cart drawer.

4. **SECTION 4: THE PROCESS ("Watch Nature Become Oil")**
   - Interactive mechanical extraction simulator:
     - Falling seeds and copra chunks into the tapered hopper
     - Rotating Vaagai hardwood mortar and pestle
     - Amber liquid conduit physics
     - Conical organic muslin filtration chamber
     - Apothecary bottle filling with liquid meniscus wave
     - Live engineering metrics (Press Temp 38.2°C, 14 RPM, Extracted Yield)
     - Play/Pause & Speed controllers (1x, 2x, 0.5x).

5. **SECTION 5: "WHY OUR OIL?"**
   - 4 Animated benefit cards with SVG line-drawing animations:
     - 100% Natural Ingredients

     - No Unnecessary Additives

6. **SECTION 6: INGREDIENTS COME TO LIFE**

   - Dynamic SVG Bezier connection lines that draw and pulse toward the central bottle on hover.

## How to Run & Preview
You can preview the website in two easy ways:

Double click or open [`index.html`](file:///d:/Siva%20Shankar/html%20sites/Ai%20Websites/05-10-2026/Home%20Made%20Oil%20Cooking/index.html) in any modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).

Run the included Node.js server:
```bash
node serve.js
```
Then visit: `http://localhost:3000`
